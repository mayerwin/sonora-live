# Code of conduct

Sonora Live is made for teaching and learning music, and its community should feel like a good lesson: patient,
encouraging and respectful, whoever you are and whatever your level.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and assume good intentions; explain rather than dismiss.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the people who use Sonora Live.
- Respect people's time, privacy and differing points of view.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information (such as a room code, an email address or a recording) without permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional or teaching setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code,
and may ban contributors temporarily or permanently. Report problems privately to the maintainer through GitHub
(for example with [GitHub's reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)),
and they will be handled confidentially.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
